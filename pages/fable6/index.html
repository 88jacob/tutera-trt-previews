How can I tell if I have low T?

Start with the test. In-office bloodwork establishes your baseline, and your provider reviews the results with you.

Is the testosterone test really free?

Yes. Your first testosterone test and consultation are free. You pay only once a provider prescribes.

What happens at the first visit?

In-office bloodwork at our Scottsdale clinic to establish your baseline, and a meeting with your provider to discuss symptoms, goals, and health history.

What is TRT?

Testosterone replacement therapy: prescription testosterone for men with clinically low testosterone, as injections or pellets. Care is provider-guided and personalized to each patient's health profile and lab results.

What if I'm not a candidate?

You pay nothing. Your first testosterone test and consultation are free, and the $149/month starts only once a provider prescribes.

Injections or pellets?

Both are in the $149/month program. You choose one with your provider.

Your provider decides after reviewing your labs and health history. Care is provider-guided and personalized to each patient's health profile and lab results.
